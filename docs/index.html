Choose a file to begin.

The first run downloads the text-recognition engine (about 10 MB), which your browser then keeps. Large scans can take a minute or two.

Works with Latin-script languages. Handwriting and very low-quality scans may not be recognised well.

“

Reading packs are often photocopies saved as pictures, so you cannot search them, copy a quote or highlight a line. Online converters want you to upload course materials to someone else's server.

Readable recognises the text on your own computer and adds an invisible text layer to the PDF, so it looks exactly the same but behaves like a normal document.

”

How It Works

Your file never leaves your computer.

1

Choose a scan

Pick a scanned PDF, or photos of pages from your phone, which become one PDF.

2

Recognise the text

Each page is read with Tesseract, the open-source text recognition engine, running inside your browser.

3

Search and highlight

Download the new PDF. It looks the same, but you can now search, select, copy and highlight the text.
